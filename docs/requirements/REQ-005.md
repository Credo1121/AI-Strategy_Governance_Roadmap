# REQ-005 — Enterprise steering in the AI era

Status: Ready for implementation. Revision 1, based on the supplied English implementation brief dated 14 October 2026.

## Goal and scope

Create an English, exactly five-slide thought-leadership deck for CIOs, business executives, IT leaders and Finance stakeholders. The deck connects strategy, investment governance, portfolio management and decentralised execution without presenting a universal organisation model or a consulting offer.

Deliver the deck as a self-contained, offline HTML presentation under `presentation/enterprise-steering-ai-era/`, accompanied by operating instructions and a source register. Do not deploy or host it.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC-01: Exactly five 1920 × 1080 slides, presented one at a time and proportionally scaled without cropping or responsive reflow.
- AC-02: The supplied titles, takeaways, argument and British English are preserved; Slide 1 displays 55% and 20% on a shared 0–100% scale.
- AC-03: Empirical observation, conceptual sources, illustrative content and original management synthesis remain visibly distinct.
- AC-04: Slides 2–5 identify the proposed nature of the model. No causal, universal, legal, ROI or fabricated client claim is introduced.
- AC-05: Slide navigation supports buttons, Arrow keys, Page keys, Home/End and hash persistence. N toggles complete presenter notes and F requests fullscreen with a graceful fallback.
- AC-06: Print CSS emits all five slides in order, one per landscape 16:9 page, with sources retained and controls/notes hidden.
- AC-07: The deck opens through `file://` without packages, servers, remote fonts, CDNs, analytics or runtime network requests.
- AC-08: Semantic slide sections, headings, labelled controls, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast and reduced-motion support are present.
- AC-09: `README.md` documents opening, navigation, notes, printing and limitations. `sources.md` contains the complete source mapping and caveats.
- AC-10: Browser review covers every slide at 1920 × 1080, scaling at 1366 × 768, interactions, offline opening and a five-page print/PDF result where tooling permits.

## Constraints and evidence limits

The source text is the source of truth. McKinsey 2025 is a dated, self-reported and correlational survey observation. SAFe and NIST are conceptual or normative references and do not validate the complete proposed steering model. The operating model, cadence and lifecycle are management synthesis. The illustrative service-assistance case contains no quantitative result.
