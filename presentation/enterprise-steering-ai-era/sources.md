## Evidence discipline by slide

1. **Empirical observation plus interpretation.** The 55% and 20% values reproduce the supplied S1 observation. The title and steering implications are management interpretation, not measured effects.
2. **Proposed management synthesis.** SAFe and NIST are conceptual references for parts of the model. The four-row decision model is not presented as an observed market pattern.
3. **Proposed operating model.** The three decision levels connect direction and evidence. Their organisational implementation depends on scale and dependencies.
4. **Proposed cadence.** The annual frame, recurring review and exception triggers are a design recommendation. Forecasting does not itself grant spending authority.
5. **Proposed investment framework and illustrative case.** The lifecycle is iterative rather than a mandatory waterfall. The service-assistance case contains no fabricated result.

## Overall limitation

This is a focused synthesis of selected empirical, normative and framework sources, not a systematic literature review. It does not establish that a specific funding architecture causes better AI performance. Architecture, data, talent, adoption and mandatory controls remain complementary requirements.
